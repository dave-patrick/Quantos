Six letterings

The Q is the same size and gap on all six, and is optically centred on each letterform's own body band. Any difference you see is the typeface alone.

How these are set

Constant geometry. The Q is 120 units square on every option, and the gap is a constant 18 units. That is what makes the six comparable.

The gap is optical, not nominal. The icon's ink is inset inside its artboard and every face gives its first letter a different side bearing — Gruppo's u alone adds 11 units. Measuring box-to-box left the real gaps running from 29 to 35 units, and Gruppo visibly further out than the rest. Each face is now positioned so ink-to-ink is identical.

Optical centring. The Q moves vertically per face, because faces don't share an x-height. Its optical centre is placed on each letterform's body band, read straight from the font files: Gruppo's x-height is 0.374 em, Anta's is 0.512. Centring on a shared line would leave the Q riding high on the small-x-height faces.

The icon's own offset is corrected too. Its ink sits 21 units below the centre of its artboard, so centring on the box centre would sit the Q low by ~2.5 units on every option.

Type sizes differ per face because each typeface has its own proportions, so the letters sit at an equivalent visual size against the Q. Shrinking the type rescales the fake-bold stroke with it, to hold the weight ratio steady.

Optical compensation. Light strokes on a dark ground bloom, so every dark stage is drawn lighter than its light twin. The numbers differ on purpose so the pair looks the same rather than merely measuring the same.
